Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about NoteMate, features, backups, and security.

Is NoteMate completely free to use? ▼
Yes! NoteMate is 100% free to download and use. You can create unlimited notes, organize with tags, search instantly, and set alarms. We also provide an affordable optional Pro upgrade for users who prefer an ad-free experience.
How are my notes secured? ▼
Your notes are stored on your local device. You can turn on Biometric Lock (Fingerprint or Face Authentication) in the Settings tab to ensure no one else can view your journal or notes even if your device is unlocked.
How does Cloud Backup and Restore work? ▼
Under Settings > Security & Sync, you can connect your Google account. Your notes and reminders will safely sync, making it effortless to restore everything whenever you change or format your smartphone.
Can I use NoteMate when I have no internet? ▼
Yes! NoteMate is built with an offline-first architecture. All core features including note editing, search, prioritization, and reminder alerts execute locally without requiring an internet connection.
How can I contact support or request a new feature? ▼
We welcome your suggestions! Simply email our developer team at professorapps.studio@gmail.com, and we will get back to you promptly.
